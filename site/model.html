Model vs market

How accurate are our models compared with the betting market? We replayed 12 past seasons match by match. Each forecast used only results from before that match's date, just as if we had made it at the time.

Season by season

Accuracy is measured by the Ranked Probability Score (RPS): 0 would be a perfect forecast, and lower is better. "Base rates" is a no-skill forecast that ignores the teams and always predicts the league's usual share of home wins, draws and away wins.

What this shows

Both models are clearly better than guessing from base rates, and the goals-based Dixon-Coles model beats Elo overall. But the market is better still, in every season. That is the expected result: Pinnacle's closing odds reflect team news, injuries and the views of professional bettors, while our models only know past scores.

Data: football-data.co.uk and openfootball. An analysis project, not betting advice.
