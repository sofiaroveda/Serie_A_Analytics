How it works

Where our predictions come from, in plain English.

Reading a prediction

Each match has a bar split into three: the chance of a home win, a draw and an away win. Wider means more likely. After the match, the part that actually happened stays bright and the rest fades.

Home win Draw Away win

A 70% favourite should still lose or draw about 3 times in 10. That is not the prediction being "wrong": football is unpredictable, and good predictions are honest about it.

How we predict

⚽

Attack and defence

Every team gets an attack rating (how much it tends to score) and a defence rating (how much it tends to concede), learned from its results. Recent matches count most, so a team's rating follows its form.

🎯

Goals, then results

From those ratings we estimate how many goals each side is likely to score, then the chance of every possible scoreline. Add those up and you get the chance of a home win, a draw and an away win.

🔄

Updated every round

After each matchday the ratings are updated with the new results, and the next round's predictions are made from them. We never use a result to "predict" a match it came from.

Our prediction record

Before each round, our predictions are saved to a public record on GitHub and never edited afterwards. GitHub keeps a history of every change, so anyone can check what we predicted and when it was published.

Want the numbers?

The Data lab puts our prediction side by side with a second model (Elo ratings) and the bookmakers' odds for every match, and shows how accurate each one has been over 12 seasons.

Data: football-data.co.uk and openfootball. An independent analysis project, not affiliated with Lega Serie A or any club. Not betting advice.
