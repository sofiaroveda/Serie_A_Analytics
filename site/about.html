About

Serie A Analytics puts a probability on every Serie A match and checks, honestly and in public, how those probabilities compare with the betting market. It is an analysis project, not a betting product: there are no tips here, and past results do not predict future ones.

What you see now

For each match, the bars show the market's view: the chance of a home win, a draw and an away win. We work these out from bookmakers' decimal odds. Odds of 2.00 imply a 1 in 2 (50%) chance. Because bookmakers build in a profit margin, the three implied chances add up to a little over 100%, so we scale them down proportionally until they add up to exactly 100%.

For finished matches we use the closing odds, the last prices before kick-off, when the market knows the most (team news, injuries, late money). We use Pinnacle's closing prices where available, since Pinnacle is a low-margin bookmaker whose prices are widely seen as the most accurate. Pinnacle odds are not available from January 2026, so from then on we use the average closing price across bookmakers, which in past seasons was typically within half a percentage point of Pinnacle's.

The Elo model

Every team has a rating. Beat a stronger team and your rating rises a lot; beat a weaker one and it rises a little. Bigger winning margins count for more. Between seasons, ratings drift part of the way back to average (squads change), and promoted teams start below average. A statistical model fitted on earlier seasons turns the gap between two teams' ratings into chances of a home win, draw and away win.

The model's settings were chosen using the 2008/09 to 2013/14 seasons only, then tested on 2014/15 to 2025/26, which it had never seen. Each prediction uses only matches played before its date. See how it did.
