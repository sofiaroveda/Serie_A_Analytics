About

An independent project putting honest numbers on Serie A.

What this is

Serie A Analytics puts a probability on every Serie A match and checks, in public, how those predictions compare with the betting market. It is an analysis project, not a betting product: there are no tips here, and past results do not predict future ones.

How the predictions work

Who built it

Sofia, a materials science and engineering student at Imperial College London, as a project in forecasting and data analysis. The code is on GitHub.

Data

Results and betting odds come from football-data.co.uk, which generously makes them available for free. Matchday numbers and the season's fixture list come from openfootball (public domain). Thank you to both.

For finished matches, the bookmakers' view uses closing odds (the last prices before kick-off): Pinnacle's where available, and the average across bookmakers from January 2026, when Pinnacle's odds stopped being published. In past seasons the two were typically within half a percentage point of each other.
